Cultural Triangle Classic

History & culture · 8 Days 7 Nights

Walk through ancient Anuradhapura, climb Sigiriya and explore the cave temples at Dambulla.

Route

Airport → Colombo → Anuradhapura → Sigiriya → Dambulla → Kandy → Airport

Day 1 — Airport → Colombo

Arrival and transfer to Colombo. Welcome dinner and orientation.

Day 2 — Colombo → Anuradhapura

Drive to Anuradhapura — Sri Lanka's first ancient capital. Visit the sacred Bodhi Tree and the towering Ruwanwelisaya Dagoba.

Day 3 — Anuradhapura → Sigiriya

Transfer to Sigiriya. Climb the iconic 5th century rock fortress. Afternoon at leisure.

Day 4 — Sigiriya & Polonnaruwa

Morning visit to the Polonnaruwa ancient city ruins. Afternoon optional Minneriya elephant safari.

Day 5 — Sigiriya → Dambulla

Visit the spectacular Dambulla Cave Temple — 2000-year-old cave murals and Buddha statues.

Day 6 — Dambulla → Kandy

Scenic drive to Kandy. Evening visit to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. Cultural dance performance.

Day 7 — Kandy

Full day Kandy city tour — Royal Botanical Gardens, Udawatta Forest, local market.

Day 8 — Kandy → Airport

After breakfast, transfer to the airport for departure.
