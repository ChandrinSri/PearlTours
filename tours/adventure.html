Adrenaline Sri Lanka

Adventure · 8 Days 7 Nights

Raft at Kitulgala, hike in the hills around Ella and finish on the surf coast at Arugam Bay.

Route

Airport → Colombo → Kitulgala → Nuwara Eliya → Ella → Arugam Bay → Airport

Day 1 — Airport → Colombo

Arrival and transfer to Colombo. Equipment briefing and adventure programme introduction.

Day 2 — Colombo → Kitulgala

Transfer to Kitulgala — Sri Lanka's adventure capital. Afternoon white water rafting on the Kelani River.

Day 3 — Kitulgala → Nuwara Eliya

Morning canyoning and abseiling. Afternoon transfer to Nuwara Eliya.

Day 4 — Adam\'s Peak

Midnight hike up Adam's Peak (Sri Pada) to watch the sunrise from the summit. Return for rest.

Day 5 — Nuwara Eliya → Ella

Train to Ella through stunning mountain scenery. Afternoon Ella Rock trek.

Day 6 — Ella → Arugam Bay

Coastal drive to Arugam Bay. Evening surf session.

Day 7 — Arugam Bay

Full day surfing, lagoon safari and beach activities. Sunset bonfire.

Day 8 — Arugam Bay → Airport

After breakfast, transfer to airport for departure.
