Catch The Nature

Wildlife · 11 Days 10 Nights

Follow rainforest trails in Sinharaja and head out on safari in Udawalawe.

Route

Airport → Negambo → Kandy → Nuwara Eliya → Ella → Udawalawe → Deniyaya → Ahungalla → Colombo → Airport

Day 1 — Airport → Negambo

Welcome to Sri Lanka. On arrival at the International Airport, you are met by our guide and driven to your hotel in Negambo. In the evening, enjoy a boat safari on Negambo Lagoon.

Day 2 — Negambo → Kandy

After breakfast, checkout from the hotel and proceed to Kandy (a UNESCO World Heritage Site). In the evening enjoy a city tour of Kandy. Overnight stay at your hotel.

Day 3 — Kandy

After breakfast, enjoy a trek at Udawatta forest. Rest of the day is at leisure. Optional: Visit the Temple of the Tooth Relic and witness a cultural show.

Day 4 — Kandy → Nuwara Eliya

After breakfast, checkout and proceed to Nuwara Eliya. En route, visit a tea factory. Rest of the day is at leisure.

Day 5 — Horton Plains

Rise early and depart by car to Horton Plains (UNESCO World Heritage Site). Trekking time: 3 to 4 hours. Return to your hotel, remainder of the day at leisure.

Day 6 — Nuwara Eliya → Ella

After breakfast, checkout and proceed to Ella. In the evening, enjoy a trek at Little Adam's Peak or Ella Rock — visit the famous Nine Arch Bridge. Overnight stay at your hotel.

Day 7 — Ella → Udawalawe

After breakfast, checkout and proceed to Udawalawa. In the evening, join your guide for a safari at Udawalawa National Park. Return as darkness falls. Overnight stay at your hotel.

Day 8 — Udawalawe → Deniyaya

After breakfast, checkout and proceed to Deniyaya. In the evening, join your guide for a site-seeing trek at Sinharaja Rainforest. Overnight stay at your lodge near Sinharaja.

Day 9 — Deniyaya → Ahungalla

Join your guide early in the morning for a Bird Watching Trek. After trekking, return to your lodge, checkout, and proceed to Ahungalla. Rest of the day at leisure.

Day 10 — Ahungalla → Colombo

After breakfast, enjoy a boat ride on the Madhu River. Return to the hotel, checkout, and proceed to Colombo. Rest of the day at leisure. Optional: Colombo city tour.

Day 11 — Colombo → Airport

After breakfast, checkout from the hotel and transfer to the airport for your departure. Safe travels!
